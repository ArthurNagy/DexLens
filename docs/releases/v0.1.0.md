The first release of DexLens, an offline-first Pokémon reference for Android.

## Highlights

- **All 1,025 species, offline from the first launch.** Browse and search by name in any language or by Pokédex
  number, and open a full entry: stats, abilities, type matchups, evolution line, forms, and moves and wild locations
  per game.
- **Camera recognition.** Point the camera at a Pokémon game, from Game Boy to Switch, and DexLens recognises the
  Pokémon from its name on screen or from its sprite, entirely on the device.
- **Optional artwork and Pokédex text.** A one-time, resumable download from PokeAPI. Everything else works without it.
- **Phones, tablets and foldables.** List-detail layout, dark theme, predictive back and TalkBack labels.
- **Private by design.** No accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. The only network use is the optional download.

## Which file do I need?

- `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk` for almost every phone from the last several years.
- `app-universal-release.apk`, about twice the size, if that one doesn't install.
- `app-release.aab` is the bundle for Google Play, and `app-mapping.txt` turns obfuscated stack traces back into
  readable ones.
